# HeroVerse: rendering and quality reference

`SYSTEM.md` is the current operating contract. This file maps the active Bible formats to their existing technical
checks. It does not impose another format's visual grammar or create a new approval stage.

## Select the actual format and profile

| Delivery | Construction and technical authority | Review and publication |
|---|---|---|
| BibleShort | `production/bible/PROMPT_GUIDE.md`, the current episode plan/import binding, `short_engine.py` and the episode's source/composed checks | Complete audio/video review ≥9.5, episode record/check-record, `publish-episode.yml` |
| Story long | `production/bible/LONG_DESIGN.md`, the long's declared frozen story criteria, `long_plan_check.py`, `long_build.py` and `qa_pilot.py` | Complete authorized review ≥9.5, `approve.yml` → `prepublish_gate.py` → `publish.yml` |

The frozen criteria files and their pinned readers remain unchanged. Existing diagnostic names R1–R16 and older
pilot criteria continue to identify their own historical checks; they are not a universal Bible-story template.
A previous format's no-person rule, 18–36-second scenery holds, title formula or thumbnail word count is not applied
to the acted Bible series. Use the current plan's declared criteria and the appropriate existing consumer.

## Shared construction requirements

- The image follows the exact words and dramatic purpose. Preserve character identity, period, recurring props
  and continuity. Inspect actual source bytes and the final crop, not only a prompt or stock description.
- Movement serves the scene. Camera-only movement is recorded as such; it does not establish acted body motion
  or speech synchronization. Preserve the required acted climax and current construction checks.
- Source rights, origin, approvals, revocations and hashes remain verifiable. Retain true licences/provider
  identity and qualify authorized source routes honestly. Public access is not a licence.
- Preserve accepted narration, speaker identity and owned music. Changed talking clips need the actual speech
  and lip-sync checks. The explicit silent Keeper Plan B has its own still-mouth evidence.
- Captions are timed to the actual accepted narration. BibleShorts use their large burned captions; the story
  long uses its recorded SRT track without burned captions. Do not transfer one format's treatment to the other.
- Use the existing decode, resolution, timing, loudness, peak, speech and visible-content checks for that output.
  A measured value is reported against the exact final file. The long's profile is not a Short duration limit.
- The approved package contains the actual title, description, thumbnail, captions, disclosure and intended
  visibility. Bible character thumbnails use approved references and at most four words under the current guide.

## Final review and completion

`ops/CLAUDE_REVIEW.md` specifies the unchanged eight-item rubric and truthful evidence. Each item must be ≥7 and
every new upload needs overall ≥9.5. The reviewer watches and hears the complete actual master. Passing technical
measures and contact sheets support that review; they cannot substitute for it.

Any actual FAIL blocks the relevant delivery. Missing evidence stays pending. Preserve the result and correct
the responsible source or construction defect; never change a frozen threshold, relabel a licence or repeat an
unchanged master to seek a better score. A draft or successful render is not an approval.

Publish through the correct existing format route, then confirm the same YouTube video is public and processed.
Retain the publication receipt and finish package retries on that video ID. The operating detail and current
continuation are in `OPERATIONS.md` and `evidence/handoff/CURRENT.md`.
