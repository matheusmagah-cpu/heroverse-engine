# Cross-platform Shorts and monetization: evidence (2026-10-20)

Owner, 2026-10-20: "Find the platform where monetization is easiest; replicate our Shorts there at the same time. Evidence, not guesswork."

Each claim is labelled **[official]** (the platform's own page or announcement) or **[3rd]** (third-party reporting). RPM figures are creator-reported and spread widely, so none of them is used as a fact.

## 1. YouTube: a deadline that changes the plan
- [official, YouTube blog, published 2026-08-10, effective 2027-02-01] The new YPP entry bar is 1,000 subscribers plus **8,000** watch hours in 365 days, or 20 M Shorts views in 90 days. Today it is 4,000 h or 10 M.
- [3rd, consistent] Channels accepted before 2027-02-01 keep their status.
- [3rd, consistent] From 2027-02-01, Shorts-pool revenue each month requires ≥ 10 M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days.
- [official] Shorts revenue = 45 % of the creator's allocation from the Shorts pool.
- **Consequences:**
  - our 4,000 h goal must be reached **and applied for before 2027-02-01**;
  - for a small channel, the money is in long-form watch time, not Shorts revenue;
  - Shorts are the funnel to that watch time.

## 2. Facebook (Meta)
- [official] Content Monetization covers Reels, videos, photos and text, and is **invite-only** (Meta, March 2026). You express interest in the Professional Dashboard.
- [official] Only original content qualifies (content you made). Your own videos may also be on other platforms: Creator Fast Track says content need not be exclusive.
- [official] Creator Fast Track pays a guaranteed monthly amount, but only to **US/Canada** creators with ≥ 100 k followers elsewhere. **We are not eligible.**
- [3rd] Countries: about 70, and Brazil appears in third-party lists. This is not confirmed on a Meta page.
- [official] The Reels Publishing API (Pages only) allows 30 posts per 24 h, 3–90 s, 1080×1920.
- [3rd, opinion] Facebook skews 35+, and churches report prayer content works there. This is not measured data.
- RPM: no official figure. Creator reports range from $0.02 to $0.20 per 1,000.

## 3. TikTok
- [official] Creator Rewards requires:
  - age ≥ 18, ≥ 10 k followers and ≥ 100 k views in the last 30 days, on a personal account;
  - videos **≥ 1 min**;
  - **original** content ("filmed, designed and produced entirely by yourself").
- [3rd, consistent] Eligible countries are the US, UK, DE, FR, JP, KR, **BR** and MX.
- [official, a different TikTok program] AI-generated content is "not original". Its status in Creator Rewards is disputed. **Our Shorts use AI images and a synthetic voice, which is a real eligibility risk.**
- [official] Content Posting API: until an audit passes, posts are **private only**; the audit takes 2–6 weeks [3rd].
- RPM [3rd, most consistent]: $0.40–1.00 per 1,000 qualified views, the highest of the group.

## 4. Instagram
- [3rd, consistent] Reels bonuses are invite-only with no published rules, so the income is unpredictable.
- Posting the same Reels uses the same Meta API as the Facebook Page.

## 5. Snapchat
- [3rd] Monetization needs 50 k followers plus view-time minimums and is invite-only. Reports say fully AI-generated videos were removed from the payout pool in 2026. **Not a fit.**

## Decision (Claude, 2026-10-20)
1. **First priority: YPP before 2027-02-01.** Long-video watch hours are the binding goal. Everything else is secondary.
2. **Facebook Page + Instagram: automatic cross-post of every approved Short** at the same moment it goes to YouTube.
   - Only Shorts ≤ 90 s, the Reels API limit (Shorts v2 tiers 0–1).
   - Also the **long prayers** as Facebook videos, the format most likely to fit an older faith audience.
   - Cost ≈ 0 with the official Meta APIs.
   - Monetization is applied for when the invite appears.
   - Go/no-go after 30 days on measured reach, followers and any earnings.
3. **TikTok: not now.**
   - Creator Rewards may exclude AI-generated videos, and the API needs a 2–6-week audit before any public post.
   - Revisit only if the Facebook test pays, or if our videos gain clearly human-made elements.
4. **Snapchat: no.**

**Owner action that cannot be avoided (once):**
- create the Facebook Page (and the Instagram professional account linked to it) under his own login;
- authorize our Meta app, which gives a long-lived Page token stored as a secret by the owner.

No platform allows an account to be created for someone else.
