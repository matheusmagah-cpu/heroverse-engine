## What this shows
1. **Production quality does not separate.** Mean watcher score: hits 6.9, dead 6.8. The worst-rated video of all
   (4.5, a static Jesus cutout) has 168K views. **Our own two videos got the two highest scores of the whole sample
   (9 and 8.5) and 6–40 views.** Quality is not what is holding HeroVerse back.
2. **The same channel, the same look, opposite fates.** Grace & Purpose: the same aerial footage, captions, cards and
   logo gave 287K (Psalm 91, male voice) and 98 views (generic "Powerful Morning Prayer…", female voice). Peaceful
   Night Prayers: the same cutout template gave 137K (64 min) and 379 views (9 min, female voice). Favor for Today: the
   same cutout, badge and captions gave 54K ("Before You Sleep, Thank God…", 16 min) and 73 views (6 min morning).
3. **Table stakes, present in all 17:** burned-in captions of the spoken words, music from 0:00, music well under the
   voice (about −15 to −20 dB), slow narration (≈ 80–120 wpm), a calm palette (dark for night, golden for morning).
   They do not create a hit; missing them is not seen anywhere.
4. **Spoken "stay with me" contracts do not decide:** 4 of 9 hits and 3 of 8 dead. Morning Prayer Today says it five
   times and has 88 views.
5. **Signals that lean one way (small numbers, HYPOTHESIS):**
   - female voice: 0 of 9 hits, 2 of 8 dead;
   - animated Subscribe pop-ups: 2 of 9 hits, 4 of 8 dead (our own reviews also found them breaking immersion);
   - a persistent "Type Amen 🙏" badge: 4 of 9 hits, 2 of 8 dead.
6. **What does separate is what the earlier tests found:** the dead videos are short (6–15 min) or carry a
   weekday or date in the title (i8i88: "Monday Morning Prayer August 24, 2026"), or have a generic search title with
   no hook. Every 60+ minute night video here is a hit.

male narration, brand in a corner, no Subscribe pop-ups. Missing: nothing among the table stakes. A persistent
