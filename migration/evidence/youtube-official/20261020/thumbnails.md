## https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431

**Add custom thumbnails on YouTube**

> Custom video and Shorts thumbnails let your audience view a quick snapshot of your content.

**Add custom thumbnails on YouTube**

> You can choose from auto generated suggested options, or upload your own if your account is verified.

**Add custom thumbnails on YouTube**

> Make sure your thumbnail follows our Community Guidelines.

**Add custom thumbnails on YouTube**

> Custom thumbnails for Shorts are currently only available to add in YouTube Studio on a computer.

**Add custom thumbnails on YouTube**

> Your account must be verified to use this feature.

**Add a custom Short thumbnail**

> For uploaded files, the recommended aspect ratio is 9:16.

**Image size & resolution**

> Your custom thumbnail image should be as large as possible.

**Image size & resolution**

> It will be used as the preview image in the embedded player.

**Image size & resolution**

> We recommend your custom thumbnails:

**Image size & resolution**

> Have a resolution of 3840 x 2160 pixels for videos and 2160 x 3840 for Shorts, with a minimum width of 640 pixels for videos and a minimum height of 640 pixels for Shorts.

**Image size & resolution**

> Be uploaded in image formats such as JPG or PNG.

**Image size & resolution**

> Remain under MB limits.

**Image size & resolution**

> Limits depend on the device you’re using to upload your thumbnail:

**Image size & resolution**

> Mobile: 2 MB for video thumbnails or 10 MB for podcasts.

**Image size & resolution**

> Desktop: 50MB for video, Shorts, and podcast thumbnails.

**Image size & resolution**

> For videos, try to upload a thumbnail with an aspect ratio of 16:9.

**Image size & resolution**

> For Shorts, try to upload a thumbnail with an aspect ratio of 9:16.

**Image size & resolution**

> For podcast playlists, upload a thumbnail with an aspect ratio of 1:1 instead of 16:9.

**Image size & resolution**

> Vertical videos with 16:9 custom thumbnails will be replaced by an auto-generated 4:5 thumbnail on the home, explore, and subscription pages.

**Image size & resolution**

> Your custom thumbnail will still appear on the watch feed, watch history, and non-mobile platforms.

**Thumbnail policies**

> All custom thumbnail images must follow our Community Guidelines.

**Thumbnail policies**

> Your thumbnails may be rejected and a strike on your account when it contains:

**Thumbnail policies**

> Nudity or sexually provocative content

**Thumbnail policies**

> Harmful or dangerous content

**Thumbnail policies**

> Repeat offenses may lead to the removal of your custom thumbnail privileges for 30 days or even account termination.

**Custom thumbnail limits**

> There's a limit to how many custom thumbnails a channel can upload each day.

**Custom thumbnail limits**

> If you get an error saying “Daily custom thumbnail limit reached” when trying to upload a thumbnail, try again in 24 hours.

**Custom thumbnail limits**

> Limits may vary by country/region or channel history.

**Custom thumbnail limits**

> Copyright strikes may impact channel history eligibility.

**Custom thumbnail limits**

> Community Guidelines strikes will affect how many custom thumbnails you can upload.

**Why are my custom thumbnails turned off?**

> YouTube may turn off custom thumbnails for certain search results when they're considered inappropriate for viewers.
