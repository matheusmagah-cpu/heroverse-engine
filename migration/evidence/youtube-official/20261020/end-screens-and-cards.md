## https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789

**Add end screens to videos**

> End screens can be added to the last 5–20 seconds of a video.

**Add end screens to videos**

> You can add up to four elements to your end screen for videos with standard 16:9 aspect ratio.

**Add end screens to videos**

> Your video has to be at least 25 seconds long to have an end screen.

**Add end screens to videos**

> Other interactive elements, like card teasers and video watermarks, are suppressed during the end screen.

**Add end screens to videos**

> End screens aren’t available for videos set as made for kids, in the YouTube music app, flash videos, mobile web, or 360 videos.

**Add end screens to videos**

> Viewers have the ability to hide end screens while watching your videos.

**What your end screen can show**

> You can add up to four elements in your end screen for videos with standard 16:9 aspect ratio.

**End screen requirements for viewers**

> Viewers also have the option to hide end screens.

**End screens and videos set as made for kids**

> End screens aren’t available for videos set as made for kids.

## https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6140493

**Add info cards to videos**

> Info cards can feature a video, playlist, channel, or link.

**Add info cards to videos**

> Cards aren’t available on videos set as made for kids.

**Add info cards to videos**

> Note: You can add up to 5 cards to one video.

**Add info cards to videos**

> Note: Message and teaser text are required for channel cards.

**How viewers can interact with cards**

> Cards are designed to complement videos and enhance the viewer experience with relevant info.

**How viewers get to cards**

> When a viewer is watching your video, they'll see a teaser for the time that you designate.

**How cards can change your content**

> Your cards won't show if your video has been claimed by Content ID and the content owner has set up a campaign.

**How cards can change your content**

> Videos displaying cards will not show a Call-to-Action overlay.

**How cards show on videos**

> Cards are shown below the video description.

**Who can see cards?**

> This feature is available to viewers on computers.

**Who can see cards?**

> Viewers can’t see cards on videos that are set as made for kids.
