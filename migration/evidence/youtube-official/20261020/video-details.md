## https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57404

**Title**

> Video titles have a character limit of 100 characters and cannot include invalid characters.

**Description**

> This section should appear after any video chapters.

**Description**

> Video descriptions have a character limit of 5,000 characters and cannot include invalid characters.

**Description**

> If the channel has any active strikes, or if the content may be inappropriate to some viewers, the corrections feature won't be available.

**Audience**

> To comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), you’re required to tell us whether your videos are made for kids.

**Age restriction**

> Age-restrict videos that may not be appropriate for certain audiences.

**Related Video**

> With advanced feature access, you can edit Shorts to include a link to a video from your channel.

**Related Video**

> The video you select has to be public or unlisted and must follow our Community Guidelines.

**Altered content**

> To follow YouTube’s policy, you’re required to tell us if your content is altered or synthetic and seems real.

**Automatic chapters**

> Any video chapters entered will override auto generated video chapters.

**End screen**

> Your video must be 25 seconds or longer to add an end screen.

## https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658

**Find playlists & videos using hashtags**

> Hashtags are keywords preceded by a # symbol.

**Find playlists & videos using hashtags**

> Hashtags allow you to easily connect your content with other videos or playlists which share the same hashtag on YouTube and YouTube Music.

**Find playlists & videos using hashtags**

> Hashtags also allow viewers and listeners to find related content via search.

**Find playlists & videos using hashtags**

> You can add hashtags to a title and description when you upload a video or record a Short on YouTube, or when you create a playlist in YouTube Music.

**Find playlists & videos using hashtags**

> Out of all the hashtags you add to the video’s description, up to three hashtags that are considered most engaging will appear by your video title.

**Find playlists & videos using hashtags**

> Your hashtags will still appear in the video description, and your videos can still appear in search results.

**Find playlists & videos using hashtags**

> Hashtags in the title and description will link to a results page featuring other videos sharing the hashtag.

**Find playlists & videos using hashtags**

> Once saved to a playlist title or description, hashtags work like clickable links.

**Find playlists & videos using hashtags**

> Hashtags in the title and description will link to a results page featuring other videos and playlists sharing the hashtag.

**Recommended Hashtags:**

> These hashtags are recommended based on a variety of signals, such as suggestions from past content uploads and, in some countries, any entered text.

**Recommended Hashtags:**

> Previously used hashtag suggestions will have a clock icon beside them to show that you have used them before.

**Hashtag use policies**

> Much like videos uploaded to YouTube, hashtags must meet our Community Guidelines.

**Hashtag use policies**

> Hashtags that violate our policies won’t show below your content, and may be removed.

**Hashtag use policies**

> No spaces: Hashtags don’t contain any spaces.

**Hashtag use policies**

> Over-tagging: Don't add too many tags in a single video or playlist.

**Hashtag use policies**

> If a video or playlist has more than 60 hashtags, we'll ignore each hashtag on that content.

**Hashtag use policies**

> Over-tagging may result in the removal of your video from your uploads or from search.

**Hashtag use policies**

> Misleading content: Don't add hashtags that are not directly related to the video or playlist.

**Hashtag use policies**

> Misleading or unrelated hashtags may result in the removal of your video or playlist.

**Hashtag use policies**

> Harassment: Don't add a hashtag with the purpose of harassing, humiliating, intimidating, exposing, or threatening an individual or group.

**Hashtag use policies**

> Violating this policy will result in the removal of your video or playlist.

**Hashtag use policies**

> Hate speech: Don't add any hashtags that promote violence or hatred against individuals or groups.

**Hashtag use policies**

> Don't add hashtags that contain racist, sexist, or other slurs.

**Hashtag use policies**

> Sexual content: Adding sexual or explicit hashtags may result in the removal of your video or playlist.

**Hashtag use policies**

> If a video is intended to be sexually provocative, it's less likely to be acceptable for YouTube.

**Hashtag use policies**

> Vulgar language: Use of profanity or offensive terms in your hashtags may lead to an age restriction or removal of your video or playlist.

**Hashtag use policies**
