## https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851

**What you need to join**

> Follow the YouTube channel monetization policies.

**What you need to join**

> Live in a country/region where the YouTube Partner Program is available.

**What you need to join**

> Have no active Community Guidelines strikes on your channel.

**What you need to join**

> Make sure 2-Step Verification is turned on for your Google Account.

**What you need to join**

> Have advanced features access on YouTube.

**What you need to join**

> Have one active AdSense for YouTube account linked to your channel, or be ready to set one up in YouTube Studio if you don’t already have one (only create a new AdSense for YouTube account in YouTube Studio—learn more).

**How you can become eligible**

> 1. Get 1,000 subscribers with 4,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months, or

**How you can become eligible**

> 2. Get 1,000 subscribers with 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days.

**How you can become eligible**

> Keep in mind that any qualified watch hours from Shorts views in the Shorts Feed won’t count towards the 4,000 qualified public watch hours threshold.

**Stay active to keep making money**

> As the YouTube Partner Program continues to grow, it's important to maintain a healthy, active ecosystem of channels.

**What if I don’t meet the program threshold?**

> If you have active Community Guidelines strikes on your channel, you can apply after your strikes expire.

**What if I don’t meet the program threshold?**

> You can also apply after you've successfully appealed to have your Community Guidelines strikes removed.

**What if I don’t meet the program threshold?**

> Current members of the program won't be removed when receiving Community Guidelines strikes.

**Qualified watch hours**

> Qualified watch hours gained from long-form videos you’ve set public

**Qualified Shorts views**

> Qualified views from Shorts you’ve set public that appear in the Shorts Feed

**If I meet the threshold, do I automatically get into YPP?**

> Your channel also must not have any active Community Guidelines strikes in order to apply.

**What happens if my counts drop below the threshold after I apply?**

> A channel needs to complete all the YPP signup steps (which currently include signing the contract and associating an AdSense for YouTube account) before they will be reviewed.

**What happens if my counts drop below the threshold after I apply?**

> If a channel is inactive and has not uploaded or posted posts for 6 months or more, YouTube reserves the right to remove its monetization.

**What happens if my counts drop below the threshold after I apply?**

> Channels will lose monetization when they violate any of the YouTube channel monetization policies.
