# Format axis: reasoned opinion and critique (owner directive, 2026-10-17)

The owner, paraphrased from Portuguese: produce content that traces a line of reasoning with opinion and critique, and mine within that structure.

**Status: CANDIDATE, pending mining.** No rule is final until mined winners, medians and weak videos confirm or reject it.

## Why it fits the goal and the policy
- Original commentary and analysis is the strongest evidence of authentic, non-templated value under the YPP inauthentic and reused content policies. Each video carries a thesis that only this channel argues, in this way.
- Mining signal so far (daily intelligence, 2026-10-17): curiosity and explanation titles ("Why did God…?", "…Explained", "The truth about…") lead long-form views per day in the niche. Prayer titles show a lift of 0.85.

## Reasoning line: the script structure to validate by mining
1. **Hook (0–8 s):** a sharp question or a common belief the video will test. It must be a promise the video keeps.
2. **Thesis:** HeroVerse's position, stated plainly in the first 30 s.
3. **Evidence:** scripture quoted exactly (KJV, public domain), with historical and literary context. Facts must be verifiable; no invented discoveries.
4. **Argument:** step by step, with an open loop carried to the next reveal.
5. **Strongest objection:** stated fairly, the way its defenders would put it.
6. **Answer:** why the thesis still holds, or where it needs nuance.
7. **Application:** what this changes for the viewer today.
8. **Payoff and CTA:** a one-line conclusion; ask the viewer to comment their view.

## Guardrails (policy)
- Critique **ideas, teachings and practices**, never people for their religion or group. Disparaging people is hateful content and not advertiser-friendly.
- No misinformation: no fake discoveries, prophecy claims presented as facts, or health and wealth guarantees.
- No impersonation of real preachers. Quotes of real people are short, attributed and commented on.
- Sensitive topics get a non-graphic framing.

## Mining plan
- **Queries:** the market panel adds 8 commentary and opinion queries, for example "what the bible really says about", "should christians", "is it biblical", "christian apologetics".
- **Daily intelligence:** flags commentary-structure titles and lists those in the top quartile of views per day.
- **AV mining:** winner, median and weak cohorts of the leading commentary channels (openings plus body windows), using the qualified instruments. The archetype is then written from that evidence and must beat the reference (BEAT_THE_REFERENCE_RULE.md).
