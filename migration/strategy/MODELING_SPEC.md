# HeroVerse: model the validated Bible-story structure

`../SYSTEM.md` is the current operating contract. This guide covers measurement and modeling; construction belongs
to `../production/bible/PROMPT_GUIDE.md` and `../production/bible/LONG_DESIGN.md`. The active formats are BibleShort and story long.
Use their current registered structures and exact criteria, not a retired format's timings or packaging.

## Evidence before a target

Identify the structure by format, length, beats, opening, voice, visual grammar, captions, CTA and packaging.
Use the validated-winner/weak-contrast rules in SYSTEM and record sources, dates, counts, instrument and limits.

- Metadata and a transcript establish only what they actually contain. They do not establish access to image,
  audio, body motion, music or delivery.
- Estimates stay estimates. Measured frame/PCM durations, counts and word rates remain distinct from a model's
  opinion. Unqualified instruments and defective transcript windows cannot supply hard production targets.
- Historical structure-mining records marked MODEL_REPORTED or informative retain that status. The archived
  inaccurate miner does not become a mandatory same-model gate for new deliveries.
- Compare equivalent quantities at comparable ages and disclose coverage. A native review's declared video
  sampling is not a claim to have examined every encoded frame.
- Apply `BEAT_THE_REFERENCE_RULE.md` to validated measurements: the target is the relevant best winner, not an
  unsupported median. Free attributes supply variation; copying a successful script is not modeling a structure.

Candidate structures remain pilots until validated. An implementation limitation is a capability question:
prove a supported zero-additional-cost delivery through the existing checks before scaling it. Do not restart a
current accepted episode to perform unrelated market research.

## Deliver the structure as an original episode

The modeling output contains the original script, its dramatic progression, source choices, technical execution
and publication package. Each choice serves the actual viewer promise and the exact words over the picture.

| BibleShort | Story long |
|---|---|
| One moment, spoken conflict opening, escalation, acted climax, payoff and original Keeper insight/close | One complete arc with its own cold open, chapters, re-hooks, climax, payoff and Keeper beats |
| Current measured 170–179-second contract; own source/timeline checks | Current registered 14–18-minute story design and declared frozen long profile |
| Episode review and `publish-episode.yml` | Long handoff/review, `approve.yml` → `prepublish_gate.py` → `publish.yml` |

The contract is implemented by the existing plan, source, preflight and publication consumers. Do not invent a
second set of hard rows from an old research table. Freeze applicable criteria before their governed render;
do not edit them after seeing a result. Preserve all still-valid inputs when continuing an existing edit.

Scripture is exact KJV with references. Original narration adds an insight and an actual narrative arc; captions,
music and movement support meaning. Retain truthful rights/provenance and character identities. Exclude fear-bait,
fabricated prophecy, health/wealth guarantees and borrowed identities or recordings.

## Review the actual result

Use the unchanged eight-item rubric in `../ops/CLAUDE_REVIEW.md`: every item ≥7, new-upload overall ≥9.5.
The reviewer receives the complete native audio/video, checks exact identity and technical/source evidence, and
grounds scores in what was watched and heard. A frame sheet or transcript alone cannot establish audiovisual
quality. Keep raw findings; correct a demonstrated defect before reviewing a changed master.

Native Gemini is authorized for the actual Short09 under the current owner decision. It is not an exclusive
reviewer chosen by an obsolete miner. Long session review uses the explicit current allowlist and records the
actual performer. A provider's authorization, response or availability never itself grants approval.

Use the existing public/processed receipt, age-matched analytics, retention and deidentified audience evidence.
Sparse observations are inconclusive. Change one supported attribute, record its expected result in the existing
learning records and evaluate it. Proven defects are fixed immediately. The strategy remains the original KJV
Bible series; a research guide does not reopen a retired niche, paid campaign or product.
