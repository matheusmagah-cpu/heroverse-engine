# pilot-001 perceptual review (2026-10-17): NOT APPROVED for publication

Reviewer: Claude Code (AI assistant) by direct visual inspection of `pilot-contact-sheet.jpg` (one frame every 20 s) and `reference-openings-sheet.jpg` (top-growth openings htx4YZVYBVs, 8EwPnMN22aQ, 17uv62CEsBQ). This is not a human review.

Automatic QA passed (structure, loudness, speech, WER, cut rate, rights), but the perceived quality and the format fit are **insufficient**:

1. The hook opens on an amateur shot: flowers in front of a brick wall and a metal pipe.
2. Shots repeat: the same flower/brick-wall clip appears in 5 of 31 sampled frames, and an AI-looking "candle with roots" clip in 4.
3. Some clips carry baked-in letterbox bars that the crop did not remove.
4. Dark forest and moss shots break the hopeful mood.
5. **Format mismatch, the main finding.** The top-growth references use one persistent praying-Jesus figure with changing backgrounds, large spoken-line captions in a text box, a title card in the first seconds, channel branding and engagement prompts ("Type AMEN", subscribe, like). Most of the measured "28–36 picture changes/min" are **caption changes, not scene cuts**. pilot-001 reproduced the statistic with a b-roll montage, which is a different product.

OCR on landscape frames produced texture noise (13/18 clips flagged); it is not a usable watermark detector for nature footage without a confidence filter.

Consequences:
- The archetype is amended: visual grammar = persistent central figure plus synchronized large captions; measured cut rate is caption cadence.
- av-profile must separate on-screen-text changes from scene changes before it is used as a production target.
- pilot-002 will follow the corrected grammar; pilot-001 stays preserved and unpublished.
