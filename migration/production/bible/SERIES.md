Scripture with our own retelling and insight. Cinematic pictures, action, voice and music serve the story.
`../../SYSTEM.md` is the operating contract; `PROMPT_GUIDE.md` defines construction.

## One story in two lengths

An arc contains four Short episodes and a long that tells its complete story. `arcs.json` records that relationship.

| Format | Editorial contract | Publication |
|---|---|---|
| BibleShort | One moment with a spoken conflict opening, escalating action, payoff, original insight and close; from part 7, measured 170–179 seconds | Own plan/sources/preflight, own complete audiovisual review ≥9.5, episode record and `publish-episode.yml` |
| Story long | The arc's whole story, 14–18 minutes under its registered design; same characters, voice and visual identity; chapters and re-hooks | `LONG_DESIGN.md`, long build/review and `approve.yml` → `prepublish_gate.py` → `publish.yml` |

Every current Short records its full narration by kind (scripture/story/insight/cta), has ≥40% original words,
an original insight of at least 20 words and a close directing viewers to the full story. Exact KJV quotations
remain Scripture even if mislabeled. `publish_episode.short_problems` checks the existing contract.
Earlier published records remain historical evidence; their duration is not a new production target.

The conflict opens through an approved character or God's exact offscreen KJV words. The Keeper appears after the
acted climax for insight and close, at ≤25% of duration; lesson time is also ≤25%. Plan B remains authorized:
silent, mouth-closed Keeper footage with the accepted narration as voice-over and real still-mouth checks.
A speaking Keeper needs passing speech/lip-sync evidence. Preserve the approved voice; do not stretch accepted
master audio to disguise a source mismatch.

Pavo is the current first route for the hook and speaking figures, with exact zero-credit verification and shared
capacity. Appropriate other scenes may use licensed/public-domain footage or GPT-generated stills with cinematic
motion, retaining true provenance and actual capabilities. The construction/review gates apply to what is visible
and heard. Camera-only movement does not establish acted body motion. No new spending.

## Packaging and the Short-to-long path

Use the same narrator, character identities, ark references and visual style within an arc. A Short's title
connects its own dramatic moment with the Bible story; a long's title identifies the complete story and human
angle. Metadata remains specific to the actual content, with exact references and synthetic-content disclosure.

Thumbnails show the story character, close-up with clear emotion and at most four words, using approved references;
part 9 onward requires the approved thumbnail. The Keeper is not the default thumbnail face.

Once the long is public and processed, `arc-links.yml` connects its public Shorts through the existing description
and comment ledger. The long links back to its Shorts and the next arc. Studio-only related-video/pinning tasks
are pending until verified. No link task or metadata retry causes a duplicate upload.
